# Delete your BeachLobbyBall account

**In the app:** open the **Profile** tab and tap **Delete account** at the bottom. Accounts confirm with their password or Google account first. This deletes your profile, friends, friend requests, invites, chat messages and the lobbies you host, removes you from other lobbies, and deletes the account.

**Without the app:** email **CONTACT_EMAIL** from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. It will be deleted, with the same data as above, within 30 days.

Reports you sent about other users' messages may be kept for moderation. Nothing else is kept.

See the [privacy policy](privacy) for what the app stores.
