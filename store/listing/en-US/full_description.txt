Tired of organizing beach volleyball in group chats? BeachLobbyBall puts every game in one place.

• Browse open lobbies at GBC, Skatås or anywhere else, and join with one tap
• Create a lobby with time, format (2v2 to 6v6), type, mode, level and whether a court is booked
• Full? Join the waitlist and get the spot when someone drops out
• Private lobbies for your crew: invite friends or share a six-character code
• Chat with everyone in the lobby
• Add the people you play with as friends, tag them privately, and invite a whole tag at once
• Play as a guest, or create an account with Google or email

Free, no ads.
